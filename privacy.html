Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

DT Collectibles AI Lister helps create, manage, and publish product listings.

Information We Process

The application may process item photos, listing titles, descriptions, pricing information, product details, drafts, and marketplace-related information you choose to use.

AI Processing

Item photos and listing information may be processed by AI services to generate listing content, product details, pricing guidance, and search terms.

eBay Account Access

When you connect eBay, authorization occurs through eBay OAuth. The application does not ask for or store your eBay password. Access may be revoked through eBay.

Use and Sharing

Information is used only to provide and improve app functionality. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process data only as necessary to operate hosting, AI, or marketplace integrations.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect credentials and application data. Sensitive credentials are intended to remain server-side.

Your Choices

You may stop using the application at any time and may revoke marketplace permissions through your eBay account.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact DT Collectibles.
